# Walkthrough — Sales Accelerator

**Pack id:** `sales-accelerator`  
**Audience:** Founder-led sales, SDRs at small teams  
**Outcome:** One outbound draft; proposal outline with assumptions; weekly pipeline rollup — **no CRM lock-in**, **no auto-send**.

## Story (fictional) — PantryCount (stock-count SaaS for cafés)

Alex (founder) sells to multi-site bakeries. Spreadsheet pipeline + discovery notes; no Salesforce required.

### Step A — Outbound draft

1. Load [`outbound-email-draft`](../../skills/outbound-email-draft/SKILL.md).
2. Use [`docs/edu/examples/outbound-email-draft.md`](../../docs/edu/examples/outbound-email-draft.md) or Prompt B/C in the skill.
3. Human copies to Gmail/Outlook and sends.

**Quickstart:** *Prospect context in → one email with clear CTA out; assumptions listed; you hit send.*

### Step B — Proposal outline

1. After discovery, paste notes into [`proposal-outline-smb`](../../skills/proposal-outline-smb/SKILL.md).
2. See [`docs/edu/examples/proposal-outline-smb.md`](../../docs/edu/examples/proposal-outline-smb.md).
3. Keep amounts `TBD` until priced; never invent ROI.

**Quickstart:** *Discovery notes → Good/Better/Best outline with assumptions and missing facts.*

### Step C — Weekly pipeline rollup

1. Paste stage rows (CSV-ish bullets OK).
2. Run [`pipeline-weekly-rollup`](../../skills/pipeline-weekly-rollup/SKILL.md) — [`docs/edu/examples/pipeline-weekly-rollup.md`](../../docs/edu/examples/pipeline-weekly-rollup.md).
3. Do **not** confuse with `weekly-ops-brief` (ops capacity/incidents vs sales stages).

**Quickstart:** *Pipeline rows → stage mix, at-risk, next actions for founder + SDR.*

## Success criteria

- [ ] Outbound draft has no fabricated savings numbers
- [ ] Proposal options include honest TBD pricing when unknown
- [ ] Rollup preserves TBD amounts and UNASSIGNED owners
