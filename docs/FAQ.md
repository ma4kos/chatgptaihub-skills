# FAQ

## Do these skills call APIs?
No. They are paste-in text workflows. Keep credentials and live integrations outside the catalog.

## Are drafts production-ready?
No. Draft entries are available for feedback and editorial promotion.

## Can a skill send email or publish?
No. Every workflow ends with human review and explicit approval.

## Where is the catalog?
Use [`marketplace.json`](../marketplace.json), or the copy served by GitHub Pages.
