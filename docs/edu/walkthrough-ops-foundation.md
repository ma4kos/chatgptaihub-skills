## Story (fictional) — Harbor Beans café group

Harbor Beans runs three cafés. Maya (GM) is tired of Discord huddles that produce no owners, and closing steps live only in her head.

### Step A — Meeting → actions (published)

1. Install [`meeting-notes-to-actions`](../../skills/meeting-notes-to-actions/SKILL.md).
2. Paste the sample in [`examples/meeting-notes-to-actions.md`](./examples/meeting-notes-to-actions.md).
3. Expect a decisions table, action rows with owners/`UNASSIGNED`, and open questions.

**Quickstart blurb:** *Paste raw huddle notes; get owners, dues, and a follow-up checklist — no CRM required.*

### Step B — Weekly ops brief (published)

1. At week’s end, collect wins / misses / risks / **stated** metrics only.
2. Run [`weekly-ops-brief`](../../skills/weekly-ops-brief/SKILL.md) with [`examples/weekly-ops-brief.md`](./examples/weekly-ops-brief.md).
3. Cap “Next week” at five items; do not invent KPIs.

**Quickstart blurb:** *Bullets in → one-page ops brief out for owners and shift leads.*

### Step C — Tribal knowledge → SOP

1. Ask Maya for closing bullets (no passwords/alarm codes in the paste).
2. Run [`sop-from-bullets`](../../skills/sop-from-bullets/SKILL.md) — see [`examples/sop-from-bullets.md`](./examples/sop-from-bullets.md).
3. Editor fills open gaps before training staff.

**Quickstart blurb:** *Turn “how we actually close” bullets into a structured SOP draft.*

### Step D — SOP → checklist

1. Feed the SOP draft into [`checklist-from-sop`](../../skills/checklist-from-sop/SKILL.md).
2. Operators tick evidence fields on shift — see [`examples/checklist-from-sop.md`](./examples/checklist-from-sop.md).

**Quickstart blurb:** *Convert a runbook into a numbered day-of checklist with stop conditions.*

## Success criteria

- [ ] One real (or fictional) meeting produced an action table with no guessed owners
- [ ] One weekly brief used only pasted metrics
- [ ] SOP + checklist loop completed on fictional data
